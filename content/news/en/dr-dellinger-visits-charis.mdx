### Quality education goes beyond textbooks and classroom lessons. It thrives when educators share experiences, exchange ideas and learn from one another. This spirit of collaboration came to life at Groupe Scolaire Bilingue <CharisName /> as the school welcomed **Dr. LaNette Dellinger, PhD**, Director of **Global Partners for Education (GP4E),** for a four-day visit from 26 to 29 October 2026.

Travelling from the Republic of Georgia to the East Region of Cameroon, Dr. Dellinger spent time getting to know the <CharisName /> community, observing classroom activities, interacting with pupils and sharing practical teaching approaches with the school's educators.

## A shared commitment to quality education

**Global Partners for Education (GP4E)** is an organisation dedicated to making quality education accessible to under-resourced communities, with a particular focus on teacher development and early childhood education. Through partnerships with schools, universities and education ministries, GP4E works to equip educators and create better learning opportunities for children. 

GP4E is one of the organisations already partnering with <CharisName />, and their commitment aligns closely with <CharisName />'s mission of creating future leaders who can positively impact their communities through quality education.

## Learning from one another

From her first interactions with the pupils and staff, Dr. Dellinger's visit was marked by warmth and curiosity. She joined the morning assembly, where she introduced herself to the children, before touring the campus and visiting different sections of the school.

She took a particular interest in how teachers manage their classrooms, including the challenges of teaching multiple grade levels together. She also observed the nursery pupils' morning routines and explored the school's music room and ICT laboratory, where discussions touched on opportunities to strengthen ICT education.

Throughout the visit, she sat in on and observed lessons across different classes and engaged teachers in conversations about their approaches to teaching. She expressed appreciation for the warm, positive relationships between teachers and pupils, particularly the way educators encourage children to recognise their own worth and abilities.

One of the highlights came when Dr. Dellinger stepped into the role of teacher herself. In a joint Class 5/6 session, she led an engaging lesson on animal conservation, using gorillas as a starting point for reading, vocabulary development and classroom discussion. Through pictures, demonstrations and interactive activities, she encouraged pupils to participate actively and learn from one another.

## Equipping teachers for more engaging classrooms

Beyond classroom observations, the visit provided an opportunity for practical professional development. During a round-table session with <CharisName /> teachers, Dr. Dellinger introduced a range of educational resources and demonstrated how they could be incorporated into everyday lessons.

Among the materials were a laminator for creating reusable teaching aids, storybooks and reading materials, hands-on mathematics resources, modelling clay, puzzles and interactive classroom tools.

She also shared ideas for making reading more enjoyable, encouraging teachers to incorporate storytelling and read-aloud sessions into their daily routines, including with older pupils.

Importantly, she reminded the educators that teaching resources and training ideas should be adapted to the realities of each classroom rather than followed as rigid instructions. Her emphasis was on creativity, practical application and making the most of available resources.

## A partnership with room to grow

Beyond the lessons and resources, the visit provided valuable opportunities to discuss how GP4E and <CharisName /> could continue working together. For <CharisName />, the visit represents more than an opportunity to receive educational resources. It is an important step towards building a meaningful relationship with an organisation that shares its commitment to improving education and developing teachers.

The school expressed its gratitude for Dr. Dellinger's generosity, time and willingness to travel such a long distance to engage directly with its community. In appreciation, <CharisName /> presented her with a few tokens of appreciation, including a school tracksuit, a commemorative plaque and school-branded mugs for her and the wider GP4E team.
