## This Monday, the CHARIS community joined schools across the region in celebrating World Teachers' Day. CHARIS teachers took part in the traditional teachers' march-past held at the Bertoua stadium, in the presence of the Governor of the East Region.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/journee-mondiale-des-enseignants-oct-5" file="IMG_5.jpeg" />

## A march-past filled with pride

Alongside teachers from other schools in the city, the CHARIS teaching team marched with pride, representing the school's values before regional authorities. It was a solemn and meaningful moment, publicly honoring the everyday work of teachers — key actors in transforming communities through education.

<GalleryFromEvent locale="en" event="2026/journee-mondiale-des-enseignants-oct-5" files={[ "IMG_6.jpeg", "IMG_7.jpeg","IMG_8.jpeg"]} />

## Back at school: a time of fellowship

Following the official ceremony, CHARIS teachers returned to school for a well-deserved moment of relaxation and fellowship, sharing refreshments and snacks together. 

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/journee-mondiale-des-enseignants-oct-5" file="IMG_12.jpeg" />

The afternoon continued with a screening of Freedom Writers, followed by a discussion on the lessons drawn from the film — particularly the power of teaching to transform lives, a theme that resonates deeply with CHARIS's own mission.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/journee-mondiale-des-enseignants-oct-5" file="IMG_14.jpeg" />

The day came to a close with a word of prayer, before everyone headed home with hearts full of gratitude and renewed motivation.
