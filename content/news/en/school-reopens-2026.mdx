## The gates of Groupe Scolaire Bilingue CHARIS Bilingual School Complex opened just before 7:00 a.m. today, marking the start of a new school year. Staff were on hand early to welcome pupils and their parents and guardians — familiar faces returning and new ones stepping onto campus for the first time.

<GalleryFromEvent locale="en" event="2026/rentree-scolaire-sept-7" files={[ "IMG_E5790.webp", "IMG_E5788.webp","IMG_E5818.webp"]} />

The warm welcome soon met an unexpected challenge: around 7:30 a.m., the skies opened up in a heavy downpour. True to the spirit of the day, reception continued regardless — staff reached for umbrellas and carried on greeting families as they arrived, rain or no rain.

<GalleryFromEvent locale="en" event="2026/rentree-scolaire-sept-7" files={["IMG_E5786.webp", "IMG_E5859.webp", "IMG_E5874.webp"]} />

By 8:00 a.m., the weather eased just long enough for the morning assembly to begin, only for the rain to return moments later, sending pupils hurrying back to the shelter of their classrooms. With the skies unwilling to cooperate, the assembly was postponed.

<GalleryFromEvent locale="en" event="2026/rentree-scolaire-sept-7" files={["IMG_E5826.webp", "IMG_E5851.webp", "IMG_E5857.webp"]} />

It wasn't until around 11:30 a.m., with the rain gone and the sun out in full, that the school finally came together for the assembly the morning had promised. The celebration opened with lively animation led by Mrs. Fopa, Mrs. Daï, and Mrs. Nguepi,

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/rentree-scolaire-sept-7" file="IMG_5890.webp" />

followed by the raising of the flag and the singing of the national anthem.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/rentree-scolaire-sept-7" file="IMG_5901.webp" />

The school's Director, Mrs. Tatiana, delivered a welcome address centered on CHARIS's values and mission,

before staff were introduced to the pupils and treated everyone to a short welcome performance.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/rentree-scolaire-sept-7" file="IMG_5924.webp" />

After the assembly, pupils headed off to break before returning to their classrooms for the remainder of the day.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/rentree-scolaire-sept-7" file="IMG_5946.webp" />

The nursery section closed at 1:30 p.m., with parents and guardians arriving to pick up their little ones, while the rest of the school continued until 3:00 p.m. The day drew to a close with a lowering-of-the-flag ceremony,

<GalleryFromEvent locale="en" event="2026/rentree-scolaire-sept-7" files={["IMG_5971.webp", "IMG_5973.webp", "IMG_5977.webp"]} />

after which pupils filed out in orderly fashion to the parents and guardians waiting eagerly to welcome them home.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/rentree-scolaire-sept-7" file="IMG_6011.webp" />

### Rain may have tested the morning, but it did nothing to dampen the spirit of the day — the first day of the 2026–2027 school year at CHARIS.
