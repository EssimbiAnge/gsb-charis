## Groupe Scolaire Bilingue CHARIS held a special ceremony this week to honor two of its pupils, **Golike Menuna Vivaldy** and **Mamang Alexandre Jr**, both finalists in the most recent edition of the National Mathematics Competition.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/remise-de-medailles-sept-16" file="IMG_6260.webp" />

### A demanding journey, capped with success

The competition, organized by the Cameroonian Association for the Promotion of Mathematics (established in 2013), unfolded over several months and multiple rounds of testing. At each stage, candidates had to demonstrate not only mathematical skill but also persistence — some advanced, others were eliminated along the way.

Golike, who was in CM2 last year and is now in secondary school, and his brother, Alexandre, who was in CE1 last year and is now in CE2, held their ground through every round. Of all the CHARIS pupils who entered, these two were the only ones to make it all the way to the final — an achievement the whole school community is proud to celebrate.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/remise-de-medailles-sept-16" file="medals.jpeg" />

### Cameroonian Association for the Promotion of Mathematics in attendance

The ceremony was attended by **Mr. Franc KENLE**, representative of the organizing association, the Cameroonian Association for the Promotion of Mathematics. He took the opportunity to remind pupils of the importance of mathematics in their education, emphasizing that the subject is for girls just as much as boys, and encouraged every pupil in attendance to start preparing for the next edition of the competition.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/remise-de-medailles-sept-16" file="IMG_6236.webp" />

### A well-earned recognition

Medals were presented to both winners by Mr. Franc KENLE himself, to applause from their schoolmates. Afterward, Mr. Michel Kombo, Golike's teacher, and Mrs. Rode, Alexandre's teacher, each presented their former pupil with a prize package, visibly proud of how far their students had come.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/remise-de-medailles-sept-16" file="IMG_6252.webp" />

### A proud parent's testimony

Alexandre's father, who attended the ceremony, addressed the audience to share his experience as a CHARIS parent. He admitted to having had some initial doubts when enrolling his son, coming from a different educational background. Today, he said, he considers himself "a happy parent," and encouraged other families in attendance to place their trust in the school, pointing to his son's and Golike's success as proof of what's possible.

<ArticleImageFromGallery locale="en" event="2026/remise-de-medailles-sept-16" file="IMG_6248.webp" />

### Looking ahead, together

The ceremony closed on a note of collective motivation, with pupils enthusiastically chanting "I'm getting ready!" and "I'm next!" as they were encouraged to start preparing for the next edition of the competition — a fitting reminder that hard work, perseverance, and consistent effort, the very qualities celebrated throughout the ceremony, are within reach of every CHARIS pupil.
